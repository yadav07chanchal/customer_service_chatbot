CUSTOMER SUPPORT FAQ

Category: Returns
Question: What is the return policy?
Answer: Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery. The product must be unused and in its original packaging.

Category: Refunds
Question: How long does a refund take?
Answer: After the return is approved, the refund is processed within 5 to 7 business days. The time taken by the payment provider may vary.

Category: Delivery
Question: How long does delivery take?
Answer: Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days after the order is confirmed.

Category: Damaged Products
Question: What should I do if my product arrives damaged?
Answer: Contact customer support within 48 hours of delivery. Provide your order details and photographs of the damaged product.

Category: Payments
Question: Which payment methods are accepted?
Answer: The example store accepts credit cards, debit cards, and UPI payments.

Category: Order Cancellation
Question: Can I cancel my order?
Answer: Orders can be cancelled before they are dispatched. Once dispatched, customers should contact customer support for assistance.

Category: Contact Support
Question: How can I contact customer support?
Answer: Customers can contact support through the help section of the website. Support staff can assist with order, delivery, payment, and return issues.

Category: Warranty
Question: What is the warranty period?
Answer: Please contact customer support to confirm the warranty period for your product.
